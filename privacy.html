Privacy

Phantom stores device preferences, saved places, and routes locally on your Mac. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated telemetry.

External services

OpenFreeMap supplies maps, EOX supplies optional historical satellite imagery, Nominatim handles submitted place searches, and OSRM handles road routing. These providers receive your IP address and the map areas, search text, or waypoints needed for those requests. Configured providers may differ.

Local exports

Session logs can contain device identifiers and coordinates. Exports stay where you save them; review them before sharing.

Removing the app

Deleting the app does not automatically remove its local settings or installed USB helper.
