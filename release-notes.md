# Phantom 1.1.0

- More reliable device sessions and clearer connection errors.
- Pause/resume, saved routes, GPX import/export, timed stops, and local session logs.
- Consistent controls and typography.
- Map/Satellite view, highway visibility control, and restrained labels at close zoom.
- Historical 2016/2017 satellite imagery with provider attribution.

Apple Silicon macOS. Ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. No automatic route restart. Public mapping providers have usage limits; walking/cycling road routes require suitable providers.
