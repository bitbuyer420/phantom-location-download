Before you download

Clear answers.

Does Phantom make an iPhone anonymous?

No. It changes app-visible Core Location for development and QA. It does not alter carrier records, cell towers, IP geolocation, emergency location, physical cameras, phone-number records, or historical account data.

Does Phantom collect my locations?

Phantom has no first-party analytics or account service. Bookmarks and recent points stay on the Mac. OpenFreeMap, Nominatim, and OSRM receive map, search, or routing requests; review the privacy notice before entering sensitive locations.

Why does macOS show an unidentified-developer warning?

This community release is ad-hoc signed and checksummed but not Apple-notarized. Control-click Phantom in Applications and choose Open once. Never disable Gatekeeper globally.

What happens when I click Reset?

Phantom stops the active hold or route, clears Apple’s location simulation service, and restores normal Core Location behavior.
